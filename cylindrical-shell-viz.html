Cylindrical Shell Element (dV)

dV = 2πr' · dr' · L

What you're seeing:

Blue shell = the thin element at radius r'
Red line = circumference = 2πr'
Green arrow = thickness = dr'
Orange line = length = L

Play with it:

Volume calculation:
Circumference = 2π(3.0) = 18.85
× Thickness dr' = 0.4
× Length L = 4.0
dV = 30.16

Why r' matters:

Try dragging the radius slider. Notice how the shell gets bigger as r' increases — more circumference means more volume, even for the same thickness!

This is why you can't just use "average density × total volume" — outer shells contribute way more volume than inner ones.
